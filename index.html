How the money works

A CCU costs the gap, not the ship

Upgrading costs target price minus donor price at today's rates — so a donor that has gone up in value since you bought it is worth more as a trade-in than it is as a melt.

Pairing does not change the total

Across a set of chains the cost is sum of targets minus sum of donors. Which donor points at which target is a decision about capability, not cost — so long as every step is legal.

Warbond means cash

Warbond pledges cannot be bought with store credit. The index shows warbond prices where they exist, but plan them as new money.

Two routes, one budget

When a buy could go two ways, keep both and mark the one you have not chosen an option. It stays costed on its own card but is left out of planned spend, so the total never counts a decision twice.

Bought is not applied

An unapplied CCU sits in your hangar and can be melted back to credit. Applying it is the step you cannot undo — so mark them bought until you actually pull the trigger.
